# Bichoku 4: sound and release candidate — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Part 1 (now, independent of the art): synthesized sound effects and a calm music loop, played by the game; the store listing texts and the privacy policy. Part 2 (after art checkpoint 2): Android Gradle build and AAB, the itch builds, the store graphics, the screenshots at the four sizes, and Juan's release checklist.

**Architecture:** The kit ships `addons/churrinche_kit/sound/synth.mjs` (Node, no packages): deterministic oscillators, envelopes, a plucked string (Karplus–Strong), seeded noise, mixing and a 16-bit mono WAV writer. Bichoku's `tools/sound/build.mjs` defines its effects and its music and writes `src/sound/*.wav` (committed; imported with QOA compression). GameScreen tells its `KitSound` which effect each action makes.

**Tech Stack:** Node 18+ (`node:test`), WAV, Godot 4.7 audio import, GUT.

**Spec:** `~/src/bichoku/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-bichoku-design.md` (Sound; Store and release); portfolio design (Sound; Release process).

## Global Constraints

- Effects: place an animal, toggle a note, clash, hint, undo, win. Music: one short calm loop, **off by default**. Generated by committed scripts; byte-identical on rebuild.
- Effects respect the effects switch, music the music switch (plan 2).
- Listing in es-419 and en-US; no donation wording in the Play listing; the privacy policy says no data is collected (offline, no network permission).
- Nothing is pushed, uploaded or signed with a real key; account steps are Juan's checklist.

## Review Focus

1. **A clash sound on every move while a clash already exists elsewhere** — clash plays only when the move just made is part of a clash.
2. **Sounds after a no-op** (tapping a given, undo with no history): no sound when the state doesn't change.
3. **The win move**: plays win, not place or clash.
4. **Loud or clicking effects** (no fade): every effect starts and ends at zero amplitude; peak ≤ 0.8.
5. **The music loop seam**: the loop's last sample flows into the first (the tail of the last notes is folded into the start).

### Task S2: Bichoku's sounds

**Files:** `tools/sound/build.mjs`, `src/sound/{place,note,clash,hint,undo,win,music}.wav` (+ `.import` with QOA compression; music loops, `edit/loop_mode=2`), `tests/tools/test_sound.sh`.

- Effects (my call, soft and short): place — a woody tock (pluck A4 + click), 120 ms; note — a light tick, 50 ms, quieter; clash — two low soft thuds, 220 ms; hint — rising three-note chime (pentatonic), 300 ms; undo — a short falling blip, 110 ms; win — a five-note plucked phrase, ≈1.3 s.
- Music: a guitar-like plucked arpeggio in 6/8 (the feel of a zamba) at 60 dotted-quarter bpm, A minor, eight bars (Am Dm G C F Dm E Am), 16 s, folded to loop, quiet.
- [ ] Shell tests: reproducible build; all seven files exist; each effect ≤ 1.5 s, music 16 s ± 0.1; peaks ≤ 0.8 (read from the WAV); first and last samples of effects are 0.
