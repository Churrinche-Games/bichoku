# Bichoku 3: art — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The engraving generator as a reusable kit library, the nine Bichoku animals drawn with it, and the game showing them — up to **art checkpoint 1** (Juan reviews the shapes). Checkpoint 2 (inks, full drawings, givens vs entered) is a later part of this plan, written after checkpoint 1 passes.

**Architecture:** The kit ships `addons/churrinche_kit/art/engraving.mjs` (Node, no dependencies): SVG path parsing and flattening, an even-odd inside test, `engrave()` (form-following parallel lines whose width follows a tone function), `band()`, outlines, and the two levels of detail. Godot never runs it (`.mjs` is not a resource). Bichoku's `tools/art/animals.mjs` draws the nine animals; `tools/art/build.mjs` writes `src/animals/art/<id>-<level>.svg`, which are committed and imported by Godot as textures.

**Tech Stack:** Node 18+ (`node:test`, no packages), SVG, Godot 4.7 SVG import, GUT.

**Spec:** `~/src/bichoku/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-bichoku-design.md` (The animals; Art checkpoints; Screen → Board); portfolio design Art direction (`~/src/game-kit/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-portfolio-design.md`); reference `~/src/game-kit/tools/art/engraving-study.html`.

## Global Constraints

- Locked style: young animals, three-quarter view, form-following width-modulated parallel lines, smooth outlined pale markings, plain dot eye with a highlight, **one ink per drawing, no tints** (pale areas are the paper `#f1e6d6`), each animal its own ink.
- Levels: **full** (56 px and up) and **simple** (40 px and below: outline ≈2.2×, line spacing ≈1.9×, eye ≈1.6×, wider markings, few bold strokes).
- Penguin and hornero are approved: port them exactly from the study, in the one-ink version.
- **Checkpoint 1:** the seven new animals at the simple level only; nothing new at full detail before Juan approves the shapes.
- Animals are told apart by silhouette first, ink second. Lobo marino upright on its front flippers, head raised (not like the penguin or the whale).
- Art is generated by committed scripts; outputs are reproducible (same script → byte-identical SVGs).

## Review Focus

1. **Two animals with similar silhouettes at 32–40 px** (penguin vs lobo marino; cóndor chick vs hornero; whale tail vs yacaré): the contact sheet shows them as black silhouettes too.
2. **Thin lines vanishing at board size on a 1× screen**: SVGs are imported at a scale that keeps the simple level's strokes ≥1 px at the board's cell size.
3. **Non-reproducible output** (unordered iteration, float formatting): build twice and diff.
4. **Path parser surprises** (relative commands, implicit repeats) in hand-written silhouettes: the parser supports M L C Q Z in absolute and relative forms and throws on anything else.
5. **Given vs entered animals** must still look different on the board with pictures instead of letters (the treatment is decided at checkpoint 2; until then entered animals are drawn smaller).
