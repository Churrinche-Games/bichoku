# Bichoku: Google Play listing (en-US)

## Title

Bichoku: Animal Sudoku

## Short description

Sudoku with nine Argentine animals instead of numbers. Free, with no ads.

## Full description

Bichoku is a 9×9 sudoku where the numbers are animals from Argentina: the jaguar, the South American sea lion, the Magellanic penguin, the Andean condor, the rufous hornero, the yacaré caiman, the Chilean flamingo, the guanaco and the southern right whale.

The rules are the classic ones: every row, column and 3×3 box holds each of the nine animals exactly once.

• Three levels: easy, medium and hard. A new board is always ready.
• Notes to mark which animals could go in a cell.
• Repeated animals are marked (or not, if you'd rather play unaided).
• A few hints per game, and your best time for each level.
• Unlimited undo.
• When you win, a fact about one of the animals.
• Engraved drawings in the style of old banknotes, made by a program.
• Spanish and English. Works offline.

No ads, no accounts and no data collection: your game is saved only on your phone.

Bichoku is free software. The source code is on GitHub.
